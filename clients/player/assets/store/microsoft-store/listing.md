## Store description

Requires the Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable. Microsoft Store installs this dependency automatically.
TVBro is a media player for IPTV services and playlists you already have permission to access.

Browse the TV guide to find something for tonight, or search for a channel,
film or series you already have in mind. Keep favourite channels close and
arrange them in the order you want.

Record a programme to watch later. Switch on timeshift to pause live TV
and return to a saved part of the broadcast. Recording needs free storage,
and your device and TVBro must stay on for scheduled recordings.

Use a keyboard and mouse at your desk or a remote from the sofa.
You can also add playlists in the web panel on your phone and connect
your TV with a pairing code or QR code.

TVBro works with M3U playlists, Xtream Codes accounts and Stalker portals.
Available channels, the TV guide and catch-up depend on your provider.

Basic playback is free. Optional paid Supporter features include movie and
series resume, recurring recordings, and automatic settings and favourites
sync. Supporter subscriptions are managed through the TVBro web panel.

Try the app before adding your own playlist. Create a local profile and
choose Try Demo streams to explore sample channels, movies and series.

IPTV subscriptions, channels, playlists, and media are not included. You must
provide your own lawful source. TVBro is not affiliated with or endorsed
by IPTV providers, broadcasters, or content owners.

## Reviewer notes

No credentials are required. On first launch, choose **English** or **Polski**
and select **Continue**. Select **Add profile**, enter any local profile name,
select **Create**, and then select **Try Demo streams**.

The Demo source provides Live, Movies, and Series tabs; generated now/next and
past programme-guide rows; simulated catch-up on the two open-movie channels;
favourites; movie detail metadata; complete season/episode browsing; and public
HLS, fMP4, HEVC, and MP4 playback fixtures.
